Shape System

Nine domains · One shape per document

Every long-form cover carries one shape from the tkxel system. The shape is not decorative and is not chosen by preference — it is determined by the document's primary subject. Select a domain to see the cover it produces.

Selection rule. Match the shape to the document's primary subject, not its secondary themes. A paper on securing AI workloads is Cybersecurity if the argument is about protection, and AI if the argument is about capability. Decide what the reader takes away, then choose.

Rules

  • One per documentNo combining, no alternating between chapters. If a topic genuinely needs two shapes, it is two documents.
  • PlacementFixed anchor across all documents in a format. Consistency of position is what makes the set read as a series.
  • RecurrenceCover and chapter openers use the same shape. The shape identifies the document, not the chapter.
  • No new shapesIf nothing fits, the subject sits outside tkxel's defined domains — a positioning question, not a design one.
